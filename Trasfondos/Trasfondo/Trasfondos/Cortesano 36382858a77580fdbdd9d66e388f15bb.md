Competencia con habilidades: Diplomacia (https://app.notion.com/p/Diplomacia-14882858a77580e79626eeba3637187d?pvs=21), Interpretar (https://app.notion.com/p/Interpretar-14882858a775809eb284c5133cd7bffe?pvs=21)
