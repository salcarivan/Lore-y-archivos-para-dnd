Habilidades: Instinto (https://app.notion.com/p/Instinto-3d482858a77580dfbd9ac03840ea9a1d?pvs=21), Persuasión (https://app.notion.com/p/Persuasi-n-3d482858a77580668f3dcec08a1410bf?pvs=21)
